Trigger avoidance and basic skin care

機轉

Foundational non-pharmacological measures: patient education, avoidance of individual triggers and irritants, barrier-supportive skin care (gentle cleanser, moisturizer), and photoprotection. Aims to reduce neurovascular/innate-immune activation and improve skin barrier function; no single defined mechanism.

核准狀態

Not a regulated drug; no approval applicable. Sunscreens and moisturizers are regulated as OTC products/cosmetics depending on jurisdiction. Moisturizers and cleansers marketed for rosacea are used as adjuncts.

適用表現

All rosacea phenotypes, especially flushing and persistent erythema, plus sensitive skin symptoms (burning, stinging, dryness); used as background for papulopustular, phymatous and ocular disease.

用法

No dose. Typical advice: cleanse once or twice daily with a gentle non-soap cleanser, apply moisturizer daily, broad-spectrum sunscreen (SPF 30-50, mineral filters often preferred for sensitive skin) every morning with reapplication every 2-4 hours when outdoors, keep a trigger diary to find personal triggers (heat, sun, alcohol, spicy food, stress, hot drinks, strenuous exercise, some skincare ingredients). Ongoing, lifelong.

療效

No quantified lesion-count or IGA effect. Benefit is expected on barrier function, comfort and reduction of flare frequency; as monotherapy it is insufficient for papulopustular or moderate-severe disease. Dietary trigger evidence is limited and individual.

常見副作用

Minimal. Some cleansers, sunscreens (chemical filters) or moisturizers can sting or cause irritant/allergic contact dermatitis; patch testing of new products is advised.

嚴重風險與禁忌

No serious risks. Avoid known irritants (alcohol-based toners, astringents, menthol, camphor, abrasive scrubs) and known contact allergens. Considered safe in pregnancy.

費用與可得性

Low cost; widely available OTC. Rosacea-specific cosmeceuticals are more expensive but not shown to be superior.

優缺點

Pros: safe, inexpensive, helps all subtypes, supports efficacy and tolerability of drugs. Cons: limited high-quality trial evidence, trigger identification is individual and burdensome, cannot control moderate-severe inflammatory disease alone.

外用 metronidazole外用藥Papulopustular rosacea (inflammatory lesions)

Topical metronidazole

機轉

Nitroimidazole antimicrobial/antiprotozoal; in rosacea the effect is thought to be anti-inflammatory and antioxidant (reduced reactive oxygen species, modulation of neutrophil activity) and possibly anti-Demodex, rather than classic antibacterial action.

適用表現

Papulopustular rosacea (inflammatory lesions); modest effect on erythema; no role in phymatous disease; limited benefit for flushing.

用法

0.75% applied twice daily or 1% applied once daily to affected areas; trials typically 9-12 weeks, long-term maintenance use up to ~6 months or longer is common.

常見副作用

Local irritation: burning, stinging, dryness, pruritus, erythema; generally well tolerated and with fewer local reactions than azelaic acid in several comparisons.

嚴重風險與禁忌

Rare contact dermatitis. Contraindicated with hypersensitivity to metronidazole/nitroimidazoles or parabens in some formulations. Systemic absorption is low; pregnancy category B in the old FDA system, use generally considered acceptable but individualize. Avoid eye contact.

費用與可得性

Low cost, generic available in many formulations in most markets; widely accessible.

優缺點

Pros: cheap, familiar, well tolerated, safe in pregnancy per most guidance, multiple vehicles. Cons: modest efficacy versus ivermectin and azelaic acid in some comparisons, low-quality older evidence, little effect on persistent erythema, twice-daily use for 0.75%.

外用 azelaic acid外用藥Mild-to-moderate papulopustular rosacea

Topical azelaic acid

機轉

Naturally occurring dicarboxylic acid; anti-inflammatory via reduced reactive oxygen species and inhibition of kallikrein-5 and cathelicidin processing, plus antimicrobial and keratolytic effects.

適用表現

Mild-to-moderate papulopustular rosacea; some effect on erythema (mainly via lesion resolution); not for phymatous or ocular disease.

用法

15% gel or foam twice daily; 20% cream twice daily; trials ran 12-15 weeks, longer maintenance used clinically.

療效

About 50-60% mean reduction in inflammatory lesions at 15 weeks, approximately 10-18 percentage points better than vehicle; Cochrane analysis found azelaic acid comparable to or somewhat better than metronidazole in lesion outcomes, with more local adverse events.

常見副作用

Burning, stinging, pruritus, dryness, scaling and erythema at application site, usually mild-to-moderate and transient; more than with metronidazole in comparisons.

嚴重風險與禁忌

Contraindicated with hypersensitivity to azelaic acid or vehicle components. Rare reports of asthma exacerbation and skin hypopigmentation with use. Pregnancy: limited systemic absorption, generally considered acceptable (FDA category B in old system). Avoid eye contact.

優缺點

Pros: proven vehicle-controlled evidence, effective for lesions, safe in pregnancy per guidance, may help post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation. Cons: more initial irritation than metronidazole, twice-daily dosing, limited effect on background erythema, higher cost than metronidazole.

外用 ivermectin外用藥Papulopustular rosacea (inflammatory papules and pustules)

Topical ivermectin

機轉

Semisynthetic avermectin; anti-inflammatory (reduced inflammatory cytokines/neutrophil activity) and antiparasitic (acts on glutamate-gated chloride channels of Demodex mites).

適用表現

Papulopustular rosacea (inflammatory papules and pustules); limited effect on persistent erythema or flushing.

用法

1% cream once daily to the face, for up to 12 weeks in pivotal trials; long-term use up to 52 weeks studied; continued as maintenance in practice.

療效

Inflammatory lesion count reduction about 76.0% and 75.0% vs 50.0% for vehicle at week 12. IGA clear/almost clear 38-40% vs 12-19% with vehicle.

常見副作用

Burning, skin irritation, pruritus, dryness; treatment-related AEs about 2.6-4.2% in pivotal trials, comparable to vehicle.

優缺點

Pros: once daily, high efficacy on lesions, superior to metronidazole in head-to-head trial, good tolerability, long-term data to 52 weeks. Cons: does not treat erythema, cost higher than metronidazole/azelaic acid, slower response in some patients.

外用 brimonidine外用藥Persistent facial erythema (non-transient)

Topical brimonidine

機轉

Selective alpha-2 adrenergic receptor agonist; causes cutaneous vasoconstriction of facial vessels, reducing persistent erythema.

適用表現

Persistent facial erythema (non-transient); does not treat papules/pustules; transient flushing not well controlled.

用法

0.33% gel (0.5% brimonidine tartrate), pea-sized amount per facial area (five areas) once daily; start with smaller amounts; effect lasts about 12 hours.

起效與持續

Onset within 30 minutes, peak at about 3-6 hours; effect wanes after about 12 hours and is not disease-modifying. Pivotal trials reported no notable rebound after stopping, but post-marketing reports describe worsening erythema beyond baseline.

常見副作用

Flushing, erythema, pruritus, burning, skin irritation, contact dermatitis; erythema/flushing AEs about 5.4% in phase 3 and 15.4% in long-term study.

優缺點

Pros: rapid onset, only vasoconstrictor class with 12-hour effect, good for event days. Cons: temporary effect, rebound/paradoxical erythema risk, irritation, cost; more rebound reports than oxymetazoline.

外用 oxymetazoline外用藥Persistent facial erythema

Topical oxymetazoline

機轉

Selective alpha-1A adrenergic receptor agonist (also some alpha-2 activity); vasoconstriction of cutaneous vessels reduces persistent erythema.

適用表現

Persistent facial erythema; no effect on inflammatory lesions.

用法

1% cream, pea-sized amount per facial area (five areas) once daily; applied to forehead, nose, each cheek, chin; long-term use up to 52 weeks studied.

關鍵試驗

Two identically designed phase 3 REVEAL trials (Kircik et al.; Baumann et al.), randomized, vehicle-controlled, 29 days of treatment then 28 days follow-up; primary endpoint at least 2-grade improvement on both CEA and SSA at 3, 6, 9, 12 hours on day 29 was met (p<0.001). A 52-week open-label safety study also performed.

起效與持續

Effect within hours (measured at 3-12 h) and lasts about 12 hours; reversal when stopped; rebound after cessation 2.2% vs 1.1% vehicle.

常見副作用

Application-site dermatitis, pruritus, pain, erythema, worsening inflammatory lesions; treatment-emergent AEs 16.4% vs 11.8% vehicle.

優缺點

Pros: low rebound rate, once daily, long-term data. Cons: modest effect size, cost, limited availability outside US, no effect on papulopustular lesions.

外用 minocycline 泡沫外用藥Papulopustular (inflammatory lesions of) rosacea, moderate to severe

Topical minocycline foam

機轉

Tetracycline-class antibiotic (minocycline 1.5%) in a hydrophobic foam vehicle; anti-inflammatory and antibacterial effects (inhibits matrix metalloproteinases, neutrophil chemotaxis and cytokine release) rather than a classic antimicrobial effect in rosacea.

適用表現

Papulopustular (inflammatory lesions of) rosacea, moderate to severe; no established effect on persistent erythema or flushing.

用法

Apply once daily to the affected facial area for 12 weeks in the pivotal trials; label dosing is once daily.

關鍵試驗

Two identical phase 3 RCTs (FX2016-11, n=751; FX2016-12, n=771), 1,522 adults with moderate-to-severe papulopustular rosacea, 2:1 randomization to FMX103 1.5% or vehicle foam daily for 12 weeks (Stein Gold et al., JAAD 2020). Both co-primary endpoints met in both trials.

療效

Inflammatory lesion count change at week 12: -17.57 vs -15.65 (p=0.0031) in FX2016-11 and -18.54 vs -14.88 (p<0.0001) in FX2016-12. IGA treatment success: 52.1% vs 43.0% (p=0.0273) and 49.1% vs 39.0% (p=0.0077). Vehicle-adjusted benefit is modest.

常見副作用

Application-site reactions (pain, irritation, pruritus), headache, nasopharyngitis; generally low incidence with no serious treatment-related events in the pivotal trials.

優缺點

Pros: topical antibiotic option with low systemic exposure, proven versus vehicle, foam vehicle suited to facial skin. Cons: modest vehicle-adjusted effect, no head-to-head versus other topicals in the cited data, cost, daily use, no effect on erythema, flammability.

口服低劑量 doxycycline口服藥Papulopustular rosacea (inflammatory lesions)

Oral low-dose doxycycline

機轉

Tetracycline-class antibiotic; at the 40 mg modified-release dose (30 mg immediate + 10 mg delayed-release beads) it acts at sub-antimicrobial concentrations, mainly by anti-inflammatory effects (MMP inhibition, reduced cytokines and reactive oxygen species) without selecting for antibiotic resistance.

適用表現

Papulopustular rosacea (inflammatory lesions); also used off-label in ocular rosacea (meibomian gland dysfunction). Not indicated for erythema or flushing.

用法

40 mg modified-release once daily in the morning, typically 12-16 weeks in trials, with maintenance beyond if needed. Off-label: 50-100 mg once or twice daily, usually tapered over weeks to months.

關鍵試驗

Two 16-week double-blind placebo-controlled phase 3 trials (studies 301 and 302) of 40 mg modified-release doxycycline, supporting FDA approval; later pooled analysis (Del Rosso et al.) and a 52-week study with randomized maintenance phase. In MVOR-1/2, doxycycline 40 mg served as active comparator (Bhatia et al., JAMA Dermatol 2025).

療效

Mean inflammatory lesion change at week 16: -11.8 (study 301) and -9.5 (study 302) vs -5.9 and -4.3 with placebo (about 61% and 46% vs 29% and 20% reduction). In MVOR-1/2 IGA success with doxycycline 40 mg was about 46.1% and 31.4% vs placebo 31.2% and 26.8% (sponsor-reported).

常見副作用

Nasopharyngitis, diarrhea, nausea, abdominal pain, hypertension, nasal/sinus congestion; photosensitivity; GI upset more common at higher doses (100 mg).

嚴重風險與禁忌

Contraindicated in hypersensitivity to tetracyclines. Avoid in pregnancy and in children under 8 (tooth discoloration, impaired bone growth). Rare benign intracranial hypertension, esophagitis, severe skin reactions, DRESS; Clostridioides difficile risk, less at sub-antimicrobial dose. Possible reduced efficacy of oral contraceptives is debated.

優缺點

Pros: strong evidence, guideline-endorsed, no antimicrobial resistance selection at 40 mg, fewer GI effects than 100 mg, long clinical experience. Cons: effect modest versus placebo, relapse after stopping, photosensitivity, avoid in pregnancy, newer oral minocycline 40 mg ER outperformed it in head-to-head trials.

口服 minocycline ER 40 mg口服藥

Oral minocycline ER 40 mg

機轉

Tetracycline-class antibiotic (minocycline hydrochloride, extended release, DFD-29) at a low dose; anti-inflammatory and immunomodulatory action (MMP and cytokine inhibition, reduced neutrophil activity); extended release intended to lower peak levels and vestibular/GI effects.

用法

40 mg once daily; trials used 16 weeks of treatment.

關鍵試驗

Two identical phase 3 RCTs, MVOR-1 (NCT05296629) and MVOR-2 (NCT05343455), about 320 adults each with moderate-to-severe papulopustular rosacea, randomized 3:3:2 to DFD-29 40 mg, doxycycline 40 mg or placebo daily for 16 weeks (Bhatia, Del Rosso, Stein Gold et al., JAMA Dermatol 2025;161(5):499-507).

嚴重風險與禁忌

Label warnings include anaphylaxis, serious skin reactions (Stevens-Johnson syndrome), DRESS (discontinue immediately), as well as typical minocycline concerns: hepatotoxicity, drug-induced lupus, autoimmune hepatitis, intracranial hypertension, pigmentation, C. difficile colitis. Avoid in pregnancy and in children under 8 (tooth discoloration, bone growth effects).

優缺點

Pros: head-to-head superiority over doxycycline 40 mg in IGA success, once daily oral, low dose. Cons: efficacy data from sponsor-funded trials of only 16 weeks, minocycline-specific risks (pigmentation, lupus-like reactions, vestibular effects), cost, new with limited real-world data, avoid in pregnancy.

低劑量口服 isotretinoin口服藥Refractory papulopustular rosacea and phymatous rosacea (including rhinophyma)

Low-dose oral isotretinoin

機轉

Oral retinoid (13-cis-retinoic acid); reduces sebaceous gland size and sebum output, with anti-inflammatory and anti-fibrotic effects. Used at about 0.1-0.5 mg/kg/day in rosacea, well below acne doses.

適用表現

Refractory papulopustular rosacea and phymatous rosacea (including rhinophyma); limited effect on persistent erythema or telangiectasia.

關鍵試驗

Sbidian et al. 2016 (PubMed 26854486): multicenter double-blind placebo-controlled RCT, 156 patients with difficult-to-treat papulopustular rosacea randomized 2:1 to isotretinoin 0.25 mg/kg/day versus placebo; low-dose isotretinoin was effective. Gollnick (conference report): 12-week double-blind trial, 224 patients with papulopustular/phymatous rosacea, arms isotretinoin 0.1, 0.3, 0.5 mg/kg/day, doxycycline, placebo; 0.3 mg/kg/day optimal, 0.1 inferior to 0.3, 0.5 no better and more irritation. Kirshen et al. systematic review and meta-analysis: 16 studies, 4 randomized, mean dose about 0.3 mg/kg/day.

常見副作用

Cheilitis, xerosis of skin, eyes and mucosa, facial dermatitis/irritation, epistaxis, elevated lipids and liver enzymes (dose-related, milder at low dose).

嚴重風險與禁忌

Highly teratogenic: contraindicated in pregnancy and requires contraception/pregnancy testing in people able to become pregnant. Other cautions: hypertriglyceridemia, hepatotoxicity, concurrent tetracyclines (intracranial hypertension risk), mood changes reported (causal link debated), bone/musculoskeletal effects.

費用與可得性

Generic isotretinoin widely available and relatively inexpensive, but prescribing is restricted by pregnancy-prevention programs and specialist oversight; use is off-label so insurance coverage may vary.

優缺點

Pros: only systemic option with RCT support for refractory papulopustular and phymatous disease; low dose reduces side effects; generic. Cons: off-label, teratogenicity and monitoring burden, mucocutaneous dryness, relapse after stopping, little benefit for erythema.

血管雷射與 IPL處置Persistent centrofacial erythema and telangiectasia

Vascular laser and IPL

機轉

Light-based devices using selective photothermolysis: chromophore oxyhemoglobin absorbs light (PDL 585-595 nm, KTP 532 nm, Nd:YAG 1064 nm, IPL broadband about 500-1200 nm), heating and coagulating ectatic dermal vessels and reducing erythema and telangiectasia.

適用表現

Persistent centrofacial erythema and telangiectasia; some reports of benefit for flushing and, with IPL, papulopustular symptoms. Not a primary treatment for phymatous disease (ablative lasers are used there).

關鍵試驗

Husein-ElAhmed & Steinhoff, Int J Dermatol 2021: systematic review with meta-analysis of 12 records; PDL had the most robust evidence, and no significant difference in erythema or telangiectasia outcomes was found between PDL and other light-based devices (Nd:YAG, IPL); evidence quality low to moderate. Martignago et al., Indian J Dermatol Venereol Leprol 2024: PRISMA systematic review of 14 IPL studies; most showed positive effects on telangiectasia and erythema with transitory adverse effects, but methodological quality was poor.

常見副作用

Transient erythema, edema, pain; purpura (especially with PDL at purpuric settings); crusting or blistering; transient pigment change.

費用與可得性

Out-of-pocket cost per session is moderate to high and often not insurance-covered when considered cosmetic; requires clinic with device and trained operator.

優缺點

Pros: treats erythema and telangiectasia that medicines do not; short sessions; minimal systemic risk. Cons: repeated sessions, cost, downtime (purpura with PDL), pigment risk in darker skin, low-to-moderate quality evidence and non-standardized protocols, recurrence.

眼型酒糟處理眼型專屬Ocular rosacea: blepharitis, meibomian gland dysfunction, dry eye, chalazia, conjunctivitis, and in severe…

Ocular rosacea management

機轉

Stepwise multimodal care of rosacea affecting eyelids and ocular surface (meibomian gland dysfunction, blepharitis, dry eye, keratitis): lid hygiene and warm compresses, lubricants, topical cyclosporine (calcineurin inhibitor, immunomodulator), topical antibiotics such as azithromycin, and oral tetracyclines (doxycycline; antimicrobial plus anti-inflammatory/MMP-inhibiting effects).

適用表現

Ocular rosacea: blepharitis, meibomian gland dysfunction, dry eye, chalazia, conjunctivitis, and in severe cases keratitis.

關鍵試驗

Randomized trial (2015, Int J Ophthalmol, 38 patients): topical cyclosporine versus oral doxycycline; both improved ocular signs, cyclosporine better for symptoms and eyelid signs. Systematic review of ocular rosacea treatment: direct comparisons lacking; pooled response rates highest for topical antimicrobials, oral antibiotics, IPL and cyclosporine as single agents.

常見副作用

Cyclosporine drops: burning or stinging. Doxycycline: gastrointestinal upset, photosensitivity. Lid hygiene: minimal.

嚴重風險與禁忌

Tetracyclines are contraindicated in pregnancy and in young children (tooth discoloration); rare intracranial hypertension, esophagitis. Severe ocular rosacea with corneal involvement needs ophthalmologist care to prevent vision-threatening complications.

優缺點

Pros: stepwise, mostly low-risk options; combination approach appears effective. Cons: evidence mostly small trials and expert consensus; long-term adherence needed; doxycycline side effects limit prolonged use; no approved ocular-specific agent.

哪些食物與習慣會讓臉更紅

多數數字來自美國酒糟協會（NRS）的病友自述調查，代表病友認為什麼會讓症狀加重，並非已證實的因果。飲食方面缺少設計良好的對照研究，也沒有適用所有人的建議，每個人的誘因不同。

食物與飲料誘因

  • 52%紅酒；烈酒 42%

    酒精（紅酒、烈酒）

    NRS 病友調查中，紅酒是最常被點名的酒類（52%），其次是烈酒（42%）；另一份 1999 年調查中紅酒為 48% 以上。酒精與酒糟風險升高有關的說法來自回顧文獻，皆非對照試驗。

    建議最保險的做法是少喝或不喝；若要喝，先觀察自己的反應。

    證據：病友自述調查；回顧文獻

  • 33%熱咖啡；熱茶 30%

    熱飲（熱咖啡、熱茶）

    NRS 調查中 33% 的人表示熱咖啡會誘發、30% 表示熱茶會。但咖啡與咖啡因的證據方向不一致：部分研究提示喝咖啡可能降低酒糟風險。

    建議若熱飲會讓你潮紅，改放涼再喝，並以自己的反應為準。

    證據：病友自述調查；觀察性研究方向不一

  • 61%受影響者中點名辣椒的比例

    辛辣食物

    NRS 2003 年調查（逾 500 人）中，受辛辣食物影響的人有 61% 列出辣椒。但有研究並未證實辛辣食物與酒糟的因果關係，推測只是加重潮紅、刺痛與灼熱感。

    建議吃辣後會潮紅或灼熱的人可以避開；沒有反應的人不必一律禁止。

    證據：病友自述調查；因果未證實

  • 以通報為主，沒有比例數據

    含肉桂醛的食物（番茄、柑橘、巧克力、肉桂）

    回顧文獻把含肉桂醛的食物列為常被通報的誘因。這類證據多來自病友通報，缺少對照研究。

    建議逐一試驗、記錄反應，不要整類食物一次全戒。

    證據：回顧文獻整理；原文未取得

  • 以通報為主，沒有比例數據

    高組織胺食物（陳年起司、葡萄酒、加工肉品）

    同一份回顧把高組織胺食物列為常被通報的誘因。證據強度與上一項相同：以通報為主，缺乏對照研究。

    建議同上；若與酒精同時出現，較難判斷是哪一個造成。

    證據：回顧文獻整理；原文未取得

應避免的生活習慣

  • 81%最常見的誘因

    日曬

    NRS 2002 年調查（1,066 人）中 81% 表示日曬會加重酒糟，63% 把它列為前三大誘因之一。

    建議每天防曬，戴帽、避開正午強光；可參考「誘因避免與基礎護理」一項的用法。

    證據：病友自述調查

  • 79%64% 列為前三大

    情緒壓力

    同一份調查中 79% 點名情緒壓力，64% 把它列為前三大誘因。

    建議壓力無法完全避免，但可留意哪些情境容易讓臉發紅。

    證據：病友自述調查

  • 75%

    炎熱天氣與高溫環境

    炎熱天氣是 75%。NRS 另有針對熱源的調查（2010），也指出暴露於熱源會使酒糟惡化。

    建議少待在悶熱空間，如桑拿、蒸氣室；外出盡量避開高溫時段。

    證據：病友自述調查

  • 57%

    風

    強風為 57%（同一份調查整理表）。

    建議寒冷有風時以圍巾或口罩保護臉部。

    證據：病友自述調查

  • 51%出處未逐一確認

    熱水澡、泡湯

    搜尋結果中有一份調查把熱水澡列為 51%，通用建議是用冷水或溫水，避免熱水池與蒸氣。這個數字我沒能確認原始出處，請當作參考。

    建議改用溫水，洗臉用微溫的水。

    證據：二手整理；較低確定度

  • 以通報為主，沒有比例數據

    劇烈運動，尤其在炎熱環境

    多數來源把劇烈運動，特別是高溫下的運動，列為誘因，但並不建議完全不運動。有來源建議改做瑜伽、游泳等低衝擊運動。

    建議選在涼爽處、強度中等、運動時備毛巾與冷水。

    證據：臨床與健康網站建議；非試驗證據

  • 以通報為主，沒有比例數據

    含酒精或刺激成分的保養品

    多份來源建議避開含酒精（特別是變性酒精）、金縷梅、薄荷腦、樟腦、濃香料的產品，以及磨砂、強效去角質。這與本報告「誘因避免與基礎護理」一項的建議一致。

    建議新產品一次只加一樣，間隔至少 48 小時再試下一樣。

    證據：皮膚科與健康網站建議；非試驗證據
